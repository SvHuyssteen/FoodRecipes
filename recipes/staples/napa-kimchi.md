# Napa Kimchi (Premade Paste, Cut Style)

**Category:** Staple · **Makes:** ~1 litre jar · **Calories:** ~17 kcal per 100g · **Protein:** ~1g per 100g
**Tags:** staple, ferment, korean, kimchi, cabbage, make-ahead, low-cal

> Mak kimchi (cut kimchi) using premade kimchi paste — the paste does the hard part, you do the
> salting and the waiting. Feeds the kimchijeon and the gochujang parcels.

## Ingredients

- 1 medium napa cabbage (~1.2kg)
- 60-70g coarse salt (~5% of cabbage weight)
- 180-240g premade kimchi paste (follow the jar's ratio: typically 150-200g per kg cabbage)
- Optional: 2-3 spring onions in 3cm lengths, 1 carrot julienned

## Method

1. **Cut:** cabbage into bite-size pieces, 3-4cm. Cut style ferments faster and is already pancake- and parcel-sized.
2. **Salt:** toss thoroughly with the salt in a big bowl, getting between the pieces. Leave 1.5-2 hours, tossing every 30 minutes. Ready when a thick stem piece bends without snapping. This pulls water out so the kimchi stays crunchy - it's texture, not seasoning.
3. **Rinse and drain:** rinse 2-3 times in cold water. Taste - pleasantly salty, not aggressive. Squeeze handfuls out and drain in a colander 15+ minutes. Drain hard; water dilutes everything.
4. **Paste:** mix cabbage (and spring onion/carrot) with the paste until every piece is coated. Gloves on. It should taste slightly too salty and punchy right now - fermentation mellows it.
5. **Jar:** pack into the fermentation jar in layers, pressing down firmly after each so liquid rises over the cabbage. Leave 4-5cm headspace - it expands and WILL overflow a full jar. Weight on top if the jar has one; airlock fitted if it has one; otherwise lid loose and burp daily.
6. **Ferment:** room temp 2-4 days. Taste daily from day 2 with a clean fork. Pleasantly sour with a fizzy edge = done. Move to the fridge.
7. **Age:** it keeps improving cold for months. Week 1 = fresh banchan. Weeks 2-4+ = sour and funky, which is exactly what the [Kimchijeon](../mains/kimchi-pancake.md) and [Gochujang Parcels](../mains/korean-gochujang-chicken-kimchi-parcels.md) call for.

## Notes

- Normal: bubbling, fizz, sour smell, cloudy brine. Thin white surface film = kahm yeast - harmless, skim it, resubmerge everything.
- Not normal: fuzzy coloured mold (blue/green/black) = bin the batch. It only really happens when cabbage sits above the liquid - pressing and weights prevent it.
- Everything that touches the jar from here on is a clean utensil, never fingers or used forks.
- The kimchi juice that accumulates is liquid gold - the kimchijeon batter needs 30ml of it.
- Scale: two cabbages fills two jars for barely more effort, and aged kimchi never goes to waste in this library.
