# Gochujang Salted Caramel Froyo (Creami)

**Category:** Dessert · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~510 kcal whole pint (~255 per serve) · **Protein:** ~23g whole pint (~12g per serve)
**Tags:** dessert, froyo, creami, greek-yoghurt, gochujang, caramel, korean, spicy, bold

## Ingredients

- 300g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 150ml low-fat milk
- 30g dark brown sugar
- 10g allulose
- 10g gochujang (start at 7g if cautious)
- 5ml vanilla paste
- 2.5ml fine salt (it's SALTED caramel - don't skimp)
- 1.25ml xanthan gum

## Method

1. In a small pan, melt the brown sugar with 15ml water over medium heat until it smells toasty-caramel, 2-3 minutes. Whisk in the gochujang off the heat. Cool 5 minutes.
2. Blend yoghurt, milk, caramel-gochujang mixture, allulose, vanilla and salt until smooth.
3. With the blender running, sprinkle in the xanthan gum slowly. Blend another 20 seconds.
4. Taste - you want sweet first, warmth at the back. Adjust before freezing; heat reads stronger once frozen and eaten slowly.
5. Pour into the Creami pint and freeze flat for at least 24 hours.
6. Process on Lite Ice Cream. Re-spin with a splash of milk if crumbly.

## Notes

- The gochujang works like chilli in dark chocolate - fermented depth plus a slow warmth against the cultured tang. Sweet-heat in the same register as the peri-peri and gochujang mains.
- Toasting the sugar is what makes it taste like caramel rather than just sweet - don't skip step 1.
- Great with the dark chocolate mix-in from the PB Frosty recipe (+25g chopped dark chocolate, ~130 kcal).
