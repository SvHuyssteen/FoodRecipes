# Mango Lassi Froyo (Creami)

**Category:** Dessert · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~460 kcal whole pint (~230 per serve) · **Protein:** ~20g whole pint (~10g per serve)
**Tags:** dessert, froyo, creami, greek-yoghurt, mango, lassi, indian, low-cal

## Ingredients

- 300g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 150g ripe mango flesh (fresh or thawed frozen)
- 50ml low-fat milk
- 15g honey
- 2.5ml stevia drops
- 2.5ml ground cardamom
- Squeeze of lime juice
- Pinch of fine salt
- 1.25ml xanthan gum

## Method

1. Blend yoghurt, mango, milk, honey, stevia, cardamom, lime and salt until completely smooth.
2. With the blender running, sprinkle in the xanthan gum slowly. Blend another 20 seconds.
3. Taste - aim slightly too sweet and slightly too much cardamom at room temp; freezing mutes both.
4. Pour into the Creami pint and freeze flat for at least 24 hours.
5. Process on Lite Ice Cream. Re-spin with a splash of milk if crumbly.

## Notes

- This is a proper lassi in frozen form - pairs with [Dal Tadka](../mains/dal-tadka.md) night as dessert.
- Mango sugar helps scoopability, so this one needs less added sweetener than the other froyos.
- Optional mix-in: diced fresh mango for texture (run mix-in cycle), ~30 kcal extra.
