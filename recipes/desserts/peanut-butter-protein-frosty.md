# Peanut Butter Protein Frosty

**Category:** Dessert · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~395 kcal whole batch (~200 per serve) · **Protein:** ~17g whole batch (~9g per serve)
**Tags:** dessert, frosty, peanut butter, low-cal

## Ingredients

- 320ml low-fat milk
- 30g powdered peanut butter (PB powder)
- 20g allulose
- 2.5ml stevia drops
- 2.5ml fine salt
- 5ml vanilla paste
- 1.25ml xanthan gum
- 15g peanut butter (mix-in)
- 25g dark chocolate, chopped (mix-in)

## Method

1. Add milk, PB powder, allulose, stevia, salt and vanilla paste to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
2. With the blender running, sprinkle in the xanthan gum slowly to avoid clumping. Blend another 20 seconds.
3. Taste and adjust sweetness - it'll taste less sweet frozen, so err slightly sweeter than you'd want at room temp.
4. Pour into the pint and freeze flat for at least 24 hours.
5. Process on the Frosty/sorbet setting. Re-spin if crumbly; add a splash of milk if needed.
6. Make a well in the centre, add the peanut butter and chopped chocolate, and run the mix-in cycle.

## Notes

- Dropping the whey cuts protein significantly (~9g vs 18g per serving). To raise it without the whey flavour, add unflavoured collagen or a second protein source.
- All low-fat milk keeps it lean and creamy; the real PB at mix-in gives a richer hit than PB powder alone.
- Frozen tastes less sweet, so taste on the sweet side before freezing.
- Allulose gives body; xanthan gum is the structural stabiliser. Retain milk (don't go pure cream) to avoid a hard over-churned block.
