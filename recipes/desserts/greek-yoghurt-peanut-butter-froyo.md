# Greek Yoghurt Peanut Butter Froyo (Creami)

**Category:** Dessert · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~590 kcal whole pint (~295 per serve) · **Protein:** ~40g whole pint (~20g per serve)
**Tags:** dessert, froyo, creami, greek-yoghurt, peanut butter, high-protein, low-cal

## Ingredients

- 300g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 150ml low-fat milk
- 30g powdered peanut butter (PB powder)
- 20g allulose
- 2.5ml stevia drops
- 1.25ml fine salt
- 5ml vanilla paste
- 1.25ml xanthan gum
- 15g peanut butter (mix-in)

## Method

1. Add yoghurt, milk, PB powder, allulose, stevia, salt and vanilla to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
2. With the blender running, sprinkle in the xanthan gum slowly to avoid clumping. Blend another 20 seconds.
3. Taste - it should read slightly too sweet at room temp; freezing mutes sweetness.
4. Pour into the Creami pint and freeze flat for at least 24 hours.
5. Process on Lite Ice Cream. It will likely be crumbly first pass - re-spin with a splash of milk until creamy.
6. Make a well in the centre, add the peanut butter, and run the mix-in cycle.

## Notes

- Greek yoghurt alone freezes chalky and icy - the milk loosens it, allulose gives body, xanthan stabilises. Don't skip any of the three.
- The cultured tang + peanut butter reads like PB cheesecake. If too tangy, swap 50ml of the milk for cream (adds ~50 kcal).
- Protein is already decent from the yoghurt; a scoop of unflavoured collagen raises it further without changing flavour.
