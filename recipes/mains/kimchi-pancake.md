# Low-Cal Kimchi Pancake (Kimchijeon)

**Category:** Main (light) · **Serves:** 1 as a light meal (2 pancakes, ~400 kcal total) · **Protein:** ~8g (see notes to boost)
**Tags:** main, light-meal, korean, kimchi, pancake, spicy, low-cal, one-pan

## Ingredients

- 150g well-fermented kimchi, roughly chopped — the older and funkier the better
- 30ml kimchi juice from the jar (flavour AND colour — don't skip)
- 60g plain flour
- 20g cornflour
- 70ml ice-cold water (colder = crispier)
- 5ml gochugaru (optional, for extra heat)
- 1 spring onion, sliced lengthways into strips
- 10ml neutral oil (5ml per pancake — measure it, don't pour)

### To serve
- Half batch [Korean Soy-Vinegar Dipping Sauce](../sauces/korean-soy-vinegar-dipping-sauce.md) (~25 kcal)

## Method

1. Stir flour, cornflour and gochugaru together. Add kimchi juice and ice-cold water, mix until JUST combined — lumps are fine, overmixing makes it bready. The batter should be thin, barely coating the back of a spoon; it's a binder for kimchi, not a pancake with kimchi in it.
2. Fold in the kimchi and spring onion. The ratio should look wrong — way more kimchi than batter. That's correct, and it's also why this version is low-cal: kimchi is ~17 kcal/100g, batter isn't.
3. Heat 5ml oil in a good non-stick pan over medium-high until shimmering. Pour in half the mixture and immediately spread it THIN with the back of the spoon — thin is the whole game with less oil; a thick pancake just steams.
4. Leave it completely alone for 3-4 minutes until the edges look lacy and set and the underside is deep golden. Flip once, press down firmly with the spatula, 2-3 minutes more. One confident flip, not many nervous ones.
5. Repeat with the remaining oil and batter. Cut into wedges (scissors are the Korean move), serve hot with the dipping sauce.

## Notes

- Cornflour + ice water is what buys crispness back after cutting the oil. A seasoned cast iron also works but non-stick is safer at 5ml.
- Protein boosts that keep it one pan: whisk 1 egg into the batter (+70 kcal, +6g protein), or scatter 100g raw prawns, chopped, over the pancake right after pouring (+85 kcal, +20g protein) — the classic haemul upgrade.
- Sad unfermented kimchi fix: stir 5ml gochujang and a drop of vinegar into the batter.
- Eat immediately — kimchijeon waits for no one. The crisp window is minutes.
- Pairs as a light dinner with the Korean Cucumber Salad (~460 kcal all-in for the full spread).
