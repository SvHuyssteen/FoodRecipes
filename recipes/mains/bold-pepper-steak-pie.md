# Bold Pepper Steak Pie with Roasted Garlic Cauliflower Mash

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~680 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~48g
**Tags:** main, beef, pie, puff-pastry, pepper, cauliflower, mash, comfort, south-african, bold

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 800g beef chuck or stewing beef, cut into 3cm chunks
- 3 large onions, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 30ml tomato paste
- 300ml beef stock
- 100ml cream
- 25ml Worcestershire sauce
- 10ml dark soy sauce
- 15ml coarse black pepper — the hero of the dish
- 5ml white pepper
- 10ml smoked paprika
- 15ml canola oil
- Salt to taste
- 15g butter
- 15g cornflour mixed with 30ml cold water (slurry)

### Pie top
- 1 sheet Woolworths ready-roll puff pastry
- 1 egg, beaten (egg wash)

### Mash
- Full [Roasted Garlic & Cream Cheese Cauliflower Mash](../sides/roasted-garlic-cream-cheese-cauliflower-mash.md) recipe

## Method

1. Pat beef dry and season with salt, half the black pepper and smoked paprika. Heat oil in Dutch oven over high heat until smoking. Brown beef in batches — don't crowd, 2-3 minutes per side until deeply crusted. Set aside. Non-negotiable for a rich gravy.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Add butter to the same pot. Cook onions 12-15 minutes until deeply golden and almost melting — longer than usual since there are no mushrooms, the onions carry the body. Add garlic, cook 2 minutes. Add tomato paste, cook 2 minutes until darkened.
3. Return beef to the pot. Add beef stock, Worcestershire sauce, dark soy sauce, remaining black pepper and white pepper. Stir well, bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook on the lowest heat for 1.5-2 hours until beef is completely tender. Stir occasionally.
4. Add cream, stir through. Add cornflour slurry a little at a time, stirring constantly, until gravy is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Taste — adjust salt and pepper. The pepper should hit you at the back of the throat. Leave to cool slightly.
5. Preheat oven to 200C. Spoon filling into individual oven-safe bowls or one large baking dish. Cut puff pastry to fit over the top with about 1cm overhang. Press edges down to seal. Brush generously with egg wash. Score the top lightly to let steam escape.
6. Bake 20-25 minutes until pastry is deeply golden, puffed and shattering. Don't underbake.
7. Make cauliflower mash while the pie bakes. Spoon a generous bed of mash onto each plate. Place pie portion on top — the gravy will spill out under the pastry lid and pool into the mash. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- 15ml coarse black pepper sounds like a lot — it is, and it's correct. Grind it yourself or use Woolworths whole black peppercorns roughly crushed. Pre-ground fine pepper loses its punch in the long braise.
- No mushrooms — the third onion cooked down long and the dark soy sauce provide the body and umami instead. The result is a cleaner, more beef-forward filling.
- The filling can be made a day ahead and refrigerated — improves overnight as flavours deepen. Make the pastry top fresh on the day.
- Woolworths ready-roll puff pastry is excellent — no shame in using it.
- Dutch oven is perfect for the braising stage.
