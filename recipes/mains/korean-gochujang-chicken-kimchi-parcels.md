# Korean Gochujang Chicken & Kimchi Rice Paper Parcels

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 1 (6 parcels) · **Calories:** ~770 kcal full serve · **Protein:** ~68g
**Tags:** main, chicken, korean, gochujang, kimchi, rice-paper, air-fryer, spicy, high-protein

## Ingredients (makes 6 parcels, one big serving)

- 240g chicken breast, finely diced
- 120g kimchi, chopped and squeezed dry
- 60g light cream cheese (10g per parcel)
- 12 sheets rice paper (double-layered, 2 per parcel)
- 2 spring onions — half through the filling, half sliced for garnish
- 5ml neutral oil for the pan, plus oil spray for the parcels

### Sauce (mix together before cooking)
- 15ml gochujang
- 10ml dark soy sauce
- 5ml gochugaru (optional, for extra heat)
- 5ml honey
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 5g fresh ginger, grated
- (Keep 5ml sesame oil separate — added at the end)

## Method

1. Mix all the sauce ingredients except the sesame oil in a small bowl.
2. Heat 5ml oil in a pan over medium-high. Sear the diced chicken until just cooked through.
3. Add the squeezed-dry chopped kimchi. Stir-fry 2 minutes to drive off moisture.
4. Pour in the combined sauce. Cook 2-3 minutes until thick and glossy and clinging to the chicken — no liquid should pool in the pan. Raw gochujang needs this cooking time to go deep and lose its harsh edge.
5. Kill the heat. Stir through the sesame oil and the chopped half of the spring onions. Spread the filling out and let it cool completely — hot filling steams the rice paper from the inside and makes it tear.
6. Soak 2 rice paper sheets together briefly (5-10 sec) in warm water until just pliable. Lay flat, add a spoon of filling and a 10g smear of light cream cheese, fold into a parcel, seam side down. Repeat for all 6. Double-layering is essential — single sheets tear and leak.
7. Mist or brush each parcel with oil. Air fry at 190C for 8-10 minutes until golden, blistered and crisp. Don't let parcels touch in the basket or they fuse. Check halfway.
8. Serve with the reserved sliced spring onions on the side.

## Notes

- Squeeze the kimchi hard before it goes in — trapped moisture is the enemy of crispy rice paper.
- Oil mist before air frying gives even, glassy crisping; a dry basket leaves chalky brittle patches.
- Light cream cheese against kimchi is a classic Korean pairing — creamy richness cutting the funk and heat.
- Filling MUST be cool before wrapping.
- Non-stick / well-oiled basket only; rice paper welds to bare metal.
- For lower cal: single-layer the rice paper (saves ~120 kcal) if your filling is dry and cooled enough to handle it.
- Serve with the [Korean Soy-Vinegar Dipping Sauce](../sauces/korean-soy-vinegar-dipping-sauce.md).
