# Lamb Kofta with Tzatziki

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~650 kcal per serve (with flatbread + tzatziki) · **Protein:** ~38g
**Tags:** main, lamb, kofta, mediterranean, middle-eastern, spice-mill, skewers, weekend, splurge

> The splurge, steak-pie tier — and the Kenwood spice mill's moment: whole cumin and coriander
> seeds, toasted and milled fresh, are the single biggest flavour upgrade a kofta can get over
> pre-ground jar spices.

## Ingredients

### Kofta
- 800g good lamb mince (ask the butcher for ~15-20% fat — lean kofta is dry kofta)
- 1 small onion, grated and squeezed dry
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 10ml cumin seeds (or ground, see note)
- 10ml coriander seeds (or ground)
- 5ml smoked paprika
- 3ml cinnamon
- 2ml allspice
- 10g salt, 3g black pepper
- Big handful fresh mint and/or parsley, finely chopped
- Skewers (flat metal ones hold mince best; otherwise shape into torpedoes, no skewer)

### To serve
- Full batch [Tzatziki](../sauces/tzatziki.md)
- 4 yoghurt flatbreads (any of the library's)
- Sliced red onion, tomato, and lemon wedges

## Method

1. Toast the cumin and coriander seeds in a dry pan over medium heat, 60-90 seconds until fragrant and just darkening (same move as the masala chips). Cool a minute, then blitz to a powder in the Kenwood spice mill with the paprika, cinnamon and allspice.
2. Mix the mince with onion, garlic, the spice blend, salt and herbs — then knead 2-3 minutes until it turns slightly tacky and sticky. That's the myosin binding; it's what keeps kofta on the skewer instead of in the fire.
3. Fry a teaspoon of the mix and taste it. Adjust salt and spice NOW, not after shaping.
4. With wet hands, shape onto skewers in flattened sausage shapes (~100g each) — or free-form torpedoes. Chill 20+ minutes to set.
5. Cook hot and fast: a ripping cast iron pan, the braai, or the Vortex at 200C — 10-12 minutes, turning for colour on all sides, to ~68-70C inside. Rest 3-4 minutes.
6. Serve on flatbreads with tzatziki, onion, tomato and lemon.

## Notes

- Don't buy lean mince — fat is flavour AND structure here. This is the splurge dish, let it be one.
- Pre-ground spices from the jar still work; toast them 30 seconds instead and lose a little of the magic.
- The taste-fry in step 3 is the professional habit that saves every seasoned-mince dish.
- The toasted spice blend scales — mill a triple batch and jar it; it's also the pork chop rub's backbone.
- Leftover kofta crumbles into tomorrow's shakshuka sauce, which is barely even a tip, more a destiny.
