# Dal Tadka

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~380 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~18g
**Tags:** main, dal, lentils, indian, vegetarian, high-protein, spiced, comfort

## Ingredients

### Dal
- 300g red lentils, rinsed until water runs clear
- 1 litre water
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 15g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
- 15ml tomato paste
- 5ml turmeric
- 5ml cumin
- 5ml coriander
- 3ml chilli powder
- 10ml canola oil
- Salt to taste
- Squeeze of lemon to finish

### Tarka
- 30ml ghee or butter
- 5ml cumin seeds
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 3 dried red chillies (or 5ml chilli flakes)
- 5ml smoked paprika
- Small handful fresh coriander, chopped

## Method

1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook 8-10 minutes until deeply golden — properly caramelised onion is the flavour base of the whole dish.
2. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 minutes until fragrant. Add tomato paste, cook 1-2 minutes until it darkens. Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric, cumin, coriander and chilli powder. Cook 5 minutes until tomatoes break down into a thick paste.
3. Add rinsed lentils and water. Stir well, bring to a boil then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook uncovered 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are completely soft and dal has thickened to a porridge-like consistency. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
4. Season generously with salt. Finish with a squeeze of lemon. Keep on the lowest heat while you make the tarka.
5. TARKA: Heat ghee in a small pan over medium-high until hot and shimmering. Add cumin seeds — they should sizzle and pop within 10-15 seconds. Add sliced garlic and cook 1-2 minutes until golden at the edges. Watch closely — it goes from golden to burnt in seconds.
6. Add dried chillies and smoked paprika, stir 10-15 seconds until the oil turns deep red. Pull off heat immediately.
7. Pour the entire tarka — hot oil, garlic, chillies and all — directly over the dal. It will sizzle dramatically. Scatter fresh coriander over the top. Do not stir — let people mix it at the table.

## Notes

- The tarka poured over at the end is what makes this restaurant quality — don't skip or rush it.
- Ghee gives a more authentic finish than butter but butter works fine.
- Dal thickens significantly as it cools — loosen with a splash of water when reheating.
- Keeps in the fridge 4-5 days and freezes well.
- To boost protein: stir through a tin of drained chickpeas in the last 5 minutes.
- Pairs perfectly with the Garlic & Coriander Yoghurt Flatbreads.
