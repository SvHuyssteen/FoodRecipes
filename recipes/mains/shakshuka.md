# Shakshuka

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~350 kcal per serve (2 eggs each) · **Protein:** ~18g
**Tags:** main, light-meal, eggs, mediterranean, middle-eastern, tomato, one-pan, cheap, breakfast-for-dinner

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 15ml tomato paste
- 10ml olive oil
- 5ml cumin
- 5ml smoked paprika
- 2ml chilli flakes (or more — this dish takes heat well)
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or coriander; optional 30g feta

### To serve
- Yoghurt flatbreads (any of the library's) or good bread, for mopping

## Method

1. Heat oil in a wide pan over medium. Onion and pepper, 6-8 minutes until soft and sweet. Garlic, cumin, paprika, chilli — 1 minute until fragrant.
2. Tomato paste in, 1 minute. Tinned tomatoes plus a splash of water, salt and pepper. Simmer 10 minutes until thickened to a sauce that holds a spoon-track.
3. Make 4 wells with the spoon. Crack an egg into each. Lid on (or foil), lowest heat, 5-8 minutes — whites set, yolks runny. Check early; they overshoot fast.
4. Feta and herbs over, pan straight to the table, bread for mopping.

## Notes

- The sauce must be properly thick BEFORE the eggs go in — eggs poached in watery sauce go everywhere.
- Cheapest main in the library and one of the fastest — eggs, a tin and half a pepper.
- With one flatbread for mopping: ~510 kcal. Extra protein: fold a tin of drained chickpeas into the sauce (+90 kcal, +4g per serve).
- The sauce base doubles as next day's base for literally anything — it IS the dish, the eggs are tenants.
