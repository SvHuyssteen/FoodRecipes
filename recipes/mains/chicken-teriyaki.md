# Chicken Teriyaki

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~500 kcal per serve (with rice) · **Protein:** ~45g
**Tags:** main, chicken, japanese, teriyaki, one-pan, quick, high-protein

> Real teriyaki is three ingredients reduced to a glaze in the pan — not the gloopy bottled
> stuff. One pan, 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thigh fillets (~500g), skinless, patted dry
- 5ml neutral oil
- Salt and pepper

### Teriyaki sauce
- 45ml soy sauce
- 30ml mirin (or: 20g honey + 15ml rice vinegar — tastes right, and it's already in the cupboard)
- 10g sugar or honey
- 5g fresh ginger, finely grated (optional but good)

### To serve
- 150g cooked rice (75g per person) or sesame cauliflower rice
- 1 spring onion, sliced, and sesame seeds

## Method

1. Stir the sauce ingredients together. Get the rice going.
2. Season thighs lightly (the sauce brings salt). Heat oil in a wide pan over medium-high; lay thighs in and leave 5-6 minutes until deeply golden. Flip, 4-5 minutes more, until just cooked through.
3. Pour the sauce into the pan with the chicken. It will bubble hard — turn the thighs through it constantly for 2-3 minutes as it reduces from liquid to a glossy glaze that coats the chicken. Pull the pan the moment it turns syrupy; ten seconds too long and it's toffee.
4. Rest 2 minutes, slice, spoon the pan glaze over. Rice, spring onion, sesame.

## Notes

- Thighs forgive the glaze-reduction stage; breast works but pull it earlier and expect less margin.
- The honey + rice vinegar mirin substitute means no specialty shopping — mirin is the only
  semi-exotic item in the whole Japanese lane and you can skip it entirely.
- Cauliflower rice version lands ~380 kcal.
- Add the [Korean Cucumber Salad](../salads/korean-cucumber-salad.md) or [Miso Soup](../sides/miso-soup.md) alongside — both near-free.
