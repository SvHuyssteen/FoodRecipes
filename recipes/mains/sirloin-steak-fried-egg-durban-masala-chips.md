# Sirloin Steak with Fried Egg & Durban Masala Chips

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~680 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~52g
**Tags:** main, steak, beef, egg, chips, durban, masala, spicy, air-fryer, south-african

## Ingredients

### Masala chips
- 500g Woolworths frozen thin-cut chips
- 10ml canola oil spray
- 5ml Durban curry powder (hot)
- 3ml turmeric
- 3ml cumin
- 3ml coriander
- 2ml chilli flakes (or more to taste)
- 3g salt
- 5g cornflour

### Steak
- 2 sirloin steaks (~200g each, at least 2cm thick)
- 10ml canola or avocado oil
- Salt and coarse black pepper
- 15g butter
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary or thyme

### Fried egg
- 2 large eggs
- 5ml butter or oil
- Salt and pepper

## Method

Timing plan: Chips take 20 min. Start them first, rest the steak while you fry the eggs, plate everything together.

1. Toast the spices first: in a dry pan over medium heat add the curry powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander and chilli flakes. Toast 60-90 seconds stirring constantly until fragrant and just starting to darken. Pull off the heat immediately and leave to cool for a minute. Mix in the cornflour and salt once cooled.
2. Toss frozen chips straight from the freezer in the spice mix until evenly coated — the cornflour helps the spices stick and crisps the coating.
3. Preheat air fryer oven with drum to 200C for 3-4 minutes. Load chips into drum (two-thirds full max), spray lightly with oil. Cook 18-22 minutes on rotating drum setting.
4. While chips are cooking, pull steaks from the fridge and pat completely dry with paper towel. Season aggressively with salt and pepper on both sides. Room temp steaks cook more evenly — 20 minutes out of the fridge is ideal.
5. At the 15-minute chip mark, get a heavy pan (cast iron if you have it) ripping hot over high heat. Add oil — it should smoke almost immediately. Lay steaks down away from you.
6. For medium rare on a 2cm steak: 2 minutes each side undisturbed. Don't touch them. In the last 30 seconds add butter, garlic and rosemary and baste continuously by tilting the pan and spooning the foaming butter over the steaks.
7. Rest steaks on a board for 4-5 minutes — non-negotiable. A rested medium rare sirloin is transformative; an unrested one bleeds out on the plate.
8. While steaks rest, fry eggs in the same pan in the residual butter over medium heat. Baste the tops with butter as they cook so whites set but yolk stays runny. Season with salt and pepper.
9. Chips should be done now. Tip out onto paper towel, taste for seasoning — hit with extra salt and chilli if needed.
10. Plate: chips down first, steak alongside, egg on top of the steak so the yolk runs when cut. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Toast the spices but not the cornflour — cornflour stays raw, it's just for coating and crisping.
- The cornflour in the masala coating creates a light crust so spices don't fall off in the drum.
- Don't move the steak while it sears — that's how you get the crust. Patience.
- The same pan for eggs after the steak means all that garlic butter goes into the eggs. Don't waste it.
- Durban curry powder varies in heat by brand — Woolworths or Rajah both work well. Taste your spice mix before coating and adjust chilli to taste.
- For two people with one air fryer, the chip timing works perfectly — they finish just as the steak comes off the rest.
