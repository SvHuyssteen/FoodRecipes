# Rotisserie Herb & Garlic Chicken with Fat-Basted Potatoes

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~520 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~48g
**Tags:** main, chicken, rotisserie, air-fryer, herb, garlic, lemon, potatoes, south-african, high-protein

## Ingredients

### The chicken — dry brine (night before if possible)
- 1 whole chicken (~1.5kg)
- 8g salt
- 3g black pepper

### The chicken — rub
- 4 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 15ml olive oil
- 10ml fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 10ml fresh thyme leaves
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 5ml smoked paprika
- 5ml butter, softened

### The potatoes
- 800g baby potatoes, halved (or medium potatoes quartered)
- 15ml olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- 5 sprigs fresh rosemary
- Salt and pepper
- Juice of half a lemon

## Method

1. Night before (or 2+ hours ahead): Pat chicken completely dry with paper towel. Rub all over with salt and pepper including the cavity. Leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
2. An hour before cooking, pull chicken from the fridge. Mix garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, paprika and butter into a paste. Work under the breast skin and rub all over the outside. Stuff cavity with squeezed lemon halves and rosemary sprigs.
3. Truss the chicken tightly — legs tied together, wings tucked. Thread onto the rotisserie spit and secure both forks firmly.
4. Toss potatoes with olive oil, crushed garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper. Spread in the drip tray or pan directly below the chicken to catch the dripping fat.
5. Set air fryer oven to 200C on rotisserie mode. Cook 60-75 minutes. At the 45-minute mark, toss the potatoes so they absorb the fat evenly on all sides.
6. Chicken is done when internal temp at the thickest part of the thigh hits 74C and juices run clear. Rest 10 minutes before carving.
7. While chicken rests, crank to 220C for 5 minutes to crisp the potatoes if needed. Squeeze remaining lemon half over potatoes before serving.

## Notes

- The overnight dry brine is what separates good rotisserie skin from great — don't skip it.
- Position the potato pan just below the spit to maximise fat contact.
- Don't crowd the potatoes or they'll steam instead of roast — single layer, cut side up initially.
- Woolworths baby potatoes work perfectly here — no peeling, good texture, hold together under the basting fat.
- Leftovers: shred the carcass the next day for chicken mayo or a wrap.
