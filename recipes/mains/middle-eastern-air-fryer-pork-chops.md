# Middle Eastern Spiced Air Fryer Pork Chops

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~380 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~42g
**Tags:** main, pork, air-fryer, middle-eastern, spiced, high-protein, quick

## Ingredients

- 2 bone-in pork chops (~250g each, at least 2cm thick)
- 15ml olive oil

### Spice rub
- 5ml cumin
- 5ml coriander
- 3ml smoked paprika
- 3ml cinnamon
- 2ml allspice
- 2ml turmeric
- 2ml chilli flakes
- 8g salt
- 3g black pepper
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- Zest of half a lemon
- 10ml olive oil

### To serve
- Squeeze of lemon
- Fresh flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- Fat-free Greek yoghurt on the side (optional)

## Method

1. Mix all spice rub ingredients into a paste. Pat chops completely dry with paper towel — dry surface means the rub sticks and you get a proper crust.
2. Coat chops thoroughly in the spice paste, getting into all the crevices around the bone. Marinate at least 30 minutes at room temperature, or up to overnight in the fridge uncovered.
3. Preheat air fryer oven to 200C for 3-4 minutes. Place chops on the static rack or shelf — not the drum. Flat placement lets the spice crust set properly.
4. Air fry at 200C for 10-12 minutes, flipping once at the halfway mark. Done at internal temp of 63C — deep mahogany crust from the spices.
5. Rest 3-4 minutes. Finish with a squeeze of lemon and scatter of fresh parsley. Serve with yoghurt on the side.

## Notes

- Bone-in chops stay juicier than boneless in the air fryer — the bone slows heat transfer and gives more margin before they dry out.
- Don't go past 65C internal — pork chops are lean and dry out fast. Use a meat thermometer.
- Cinnamon and allspice are the backbone of the Middle Eastern profile — don't skip them.
- Pairs perfectly with Crispy Spiced Chickpeas or the Crispy Herbed Rosti.
- Leftover meat works brilliantly in a wrap with Greek yoghurt flatbreads the next day.
