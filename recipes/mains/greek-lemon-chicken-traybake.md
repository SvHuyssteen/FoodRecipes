# Greek Lemon Chicken & Potato Traybake

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~550 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~45g
**Tags:** main, chicken, greek, mediterranean, traybake, air-fryer, one-tray, potatoes

> The Mediterranean cousin of the rotisserie-and-potatoes formula: everything on one tray,
> potatoes roasting in the chicken's fat and lemon. Near-zero washing up.

## Ingredients

- 8 chicken thighs, bone-in skin-on (~1.2kg)
- 800g potatoes, cut into chunky wedges
- 2 lemons — juice of both, one squeezed-out half thrown on the tray
- 60ml olive oil
- 15ml dried oregano
- 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- 5ml smoked paprika
- 200ml chicken stock
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional finish: crumbled feta and chopped parsley

## Method

1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper. Toss the chicken and potatoes through it in the tray itself.
2. Arrange: potatoes in a single-ish layer, chicken skin side UP on top of them, garlic and the spent lemon half tucked in. Pour the stock into the corner of the tray — around, not over, the chicken (wet skin doesn't crisp).
3. Into the Vortex at 190C for 40-45 minutes until the skin is deep golden and the thighs hit 74C. The potatoes below braise in stock and drippings while the chicken roasts.
4. If the potatoes need more colour: lift the chicken off to rest, crank to 220C and give the tray 5-8 minutes.
5. Feta and parsley over the top, pan juices spooned over everything.

## Notes

- Same fat-basting logic as the rotisserie recipe — the potatoes under the chicken are the best thing on the tray.
- Half-stock-braised, half-roasted potatoes are the Greek signature here: creamy bottoms, crisp tops.
- Leftover chicken slots straight into the [Peri-Peri Chicken Salad](../salads/peri-peri-chicken-salad.md) formula (swap peri-peri for oregano + lemon in the dressing).
- Leaner: skinless thighs and 40ml oil lands ~450 kcal.
