# Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~480 kcal per serve (with tzatziki) · **Protein:** ~45g
**Tags:** main, chicken, greek, mediterranean, skewers, air-fryer, high-protein

> The Friday feast anchor: souvlaki + [Tzatziki](../sauces/tzatziki.md) + a flatbread + your
> Greek salad is a full spread at ~700 kcal.

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts (~400g), cut into 3cm chunks
- 15ml olive oil
- Juice and zest of 1 lemon
- 10ml dried oregano
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 5ml smoked paprika
- 8g salt, black pepper
- Skewers (metal, or soaked wooden)

### To serve
- 1/2 batch [Tzatziki](../sauces/tzatziki.md)
- Lemon wedges

## Method

1. Mix oil, lemon juice and zest, oregano, garlic, paprika, salt and pepper. Toss the chicken through it. 30 minutes at room temp minimum; a few hours in the fridge is better.
2. Thread onto skewers snugly but not crushed together.
3. Air fry at 200C for 10-12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and 70C inside. (Or a ripping pan / the braai — anything hot and fast.)
4. Rest 3 minutes. Serve over or beside the tzatziki with lemon wedges.

## Notes

- Lemon in the marinade does start "cooking" the chicken — don't marinate past ~8 hours or the texture goes woolly.
- Thighs instead of breast: +60 kcal, more forgiving, arguably better.
- The full Friday spread: souvlaki + tzatziki + 1 yoghurt flatbread + Greek salad ≈ 700 kcal — feast feeling, weeknight numbers.
