# Pepperdew, Feta & Pepperoni Flatbread

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 1 per flatbread · **Calories:** ~590 kcal per flatbread · **Protein:** ~18g
**Tags:** main, flatbread, pepperdew, feta, pepperoni, quick, pizza-style, south-african

## Ingredients (per flatbread)

- 1 [Garlic & Coriander Yoghurt Flatbread](../breads/garlic-coriander-yoghurt-flatbreads.md)
- 30g cream cheese
- 40g feta, crumbled
- 50g pepperdews, sliced (keep the brine)
- 40g pepperoni, sliced
- 20g fresh rocket
- 5ml olive oil
- Squeeze of lemon
- Crack of black pepper
- Splash of pepperdew brine to finish

## Method

1. Cook the flatbread as per the library recipe — dry pan, medium-high heat, 1-2 minutes a side until blistered and charred in spots.
2. While still hot, spread cream cheese over the flatbread as the base — the residual heat softens it and helps it spread easily.
3. Scatter crumbled feta, sliced pepperdews and pepperoni over the top.
4. For a pizza-style finish: slide under the grill for 3-4 minutes until the feta colours slightly and the pepperoni crisps at the edges. Skip this step if you want it quicker.
5. Dress rocket with olive oil and a squeeze of lemon. Pile on top.
6. Finish with a crack of black pepper and a small splash of pepperdew brine over everything.

## Notes

- The pepperdew brine is the secret finishing touch — sweet, tangy and slightly spicy. Don't skip it.
- Cream cheese base acts like a richer, more forgiving pizza sauce — holds toppings without making the flatbread soggy.
- One flatbread is a solid light meal; two with a simple Greek salad is a proper dinner.
- Works equally well with the plain Two-Ingredient Flatbread if you want a more neutral base.
- Swap pepperoni for leftover rotisserie chicken for a lighter version (~480 kcal).
