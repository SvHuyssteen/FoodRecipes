# Oyakodon (Chicken & Egg Rice Bowl)

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~550 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~35g
**Tags:** main, chicken, egg, japanese, rice-bowl, one-pan, quick, comfort

> The great Japanese weeknight dish — chicken and softly-set egg in a sweet-soy broth over
> rice, 20 minutes, one pan. This is the "it's Tuesday and I'm tired" slot.

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken thigh fillets (~250g), cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 3 large eggs, LIGHTLY beaten — streaky, not uniform
- 150ml chicken stock (dashi if you ever stock it)
- 30ml soy sauce
- 15ml mirin (or 10g honey + 10ml rice vinegar)
- 5g sugar
- 300g cooked rice (150g per bowl)
- 1 spring onion, sliced

## Method

1. Bring stock, soy, mirin and sugar to a simmer in a smallish pan (snug pan = right broth depth).
2. Add onion, simmer 3-4 minutes until softening. Add chicken pieces in a single layer, simmer 5-6 minutes until just cooked through.
3. Pour the eggs over everything in a spiral — do NOT stir. Lid on, 1-2 minutes until the egg is barely set: soft, custardy, a little loose on top. It keeps cooking in the bowl.
4. Slide the whole thing over bowls of hot rice, broth included. Spring onion over. Eat immediately.

## Notes

- The egg texture is the entire dish — pull it while it still looks slightly underdone. Fully-set scrambled egg means you've made something else.
- Lightly beaten = ribbons of white and yolk through the broth, which is correct.
- Chilli flakes or a drop of sesame oil on top are non-traditional and good.
- Leaner version: 100g rice per bowl and 2 eggs lands ~430 kcal.
