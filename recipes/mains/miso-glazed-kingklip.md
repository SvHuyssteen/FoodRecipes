# Miso-Glazed Kingklip

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~350 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~35g
**Tags:** main, fish, kingklip, japanese, miso, air-fryer, quick, high-protein, low-cal

> The saikyo-yaki move — Japan does it with black cod; kingklip is firm, white and sweet, which
> makes it a closer match than salmon ever was. The occasional-nice-dinner fish.

## Ingredients

- 2 kingklip fillets (~180g each)
- 1/2 batch [All-Purpose Miso Glaze](../sauces/miso-glaze.md)
- 5ml neutral oil
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced, and toasted sesame seeds to finish

## Method

1. Pat the fillets completely dry. Brush with a THIN layer of glaze (save most of it) and rest 15-30 minutes if you have the time — even a short marinade deepens it.
2. Line the Vortex tray with lightly oiled foil or baking paper (glaze welds fish to bare racks). Air fry at 200C for 6-7 minutes.
3. Brush generously with the remaining glaze and give it 2-3 more minutes until the glaze bubbles and catches dark at the edges. It should look slightly scorched in spots — that's the flavour.
4. Done when the fish flakes at the thickest point. Scatter spring onion and sesame over. Serve with rice and the miso soup, or cauliflower rice on leaner days.

## Notes

- Glaze late, not early — miso and honey burn long before fish cooks through.
- Kingklip's firmness is why it's the glazing fish; it holds together where hake would collapse. Yellowtail also excellent here.
- With 100g rice: ~530 kcal. With sesame cauliflower rice (see peri-peri pork): ~400.
