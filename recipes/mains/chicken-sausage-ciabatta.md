# Chicken Sausage Ciabatta with Pepperdew & Bell Pepper

**Category:** Main · **Serves:** 1 · **Calories:** ~990 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~45g
**Tags:** main, chicken, sausage, sandwich, ciabatta, pepperdew, bell pepper, quick, lunch

## Ingredients

- 2 Woolworths free range chicken braai sausages (~300g)
- 1 ciabatta roll, sliced
- 1 bell pepper (any colour), sliced into strips
- 50g pepperdews, sliced
- 30ml Carb Smart mayo
- 2 slices Woolworths light cheddar or gouda
- Handful fresh rocket or baby spinach
- 1 medium tomato, sliced
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- Woolworths habanero peri peri sauce to taste
- 5ml olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add bell pepper strips, season with salt and pepper, cook 8-10 minutes until soft and slightly charred at the edges. Set aside.
2. In the same pan over medium heat, cook chicken sausages whole — don't prick them. Turn regularly for 12-15 minutes until cooked through and golden all over. Don't use high heat — chicken sausages burn on the outside before cooking through. A splash of water with a lid on for the first 5 minutes steams them through, then remove lid to colour.
3. Rest sausages 2 minutes then slice lengthways — this helps them sit flat in the ciabatta and gives you more surface area.
4. Toast the ciabatta cut side down in the same pan for 1-2 minutes until golden and crisp.
5. Build the sandwich: spread Carb Smart mayo on both sides of the ciabatta. Layer rocket, tomato, red onion, charred bell peppers and pepperdews on the base. Lay sliced sausages on top, add cheese slices, finish with a good hit of peri peri sauce.
6. Press together, cut diagonally and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Don't prick the sausages — keeps the juices in and stops them drying out.
- Medium heat is essential for chicken sausages — they burn before cooking through on high.
- The pepperdew brine drizzled inside adds extra tang — worth doing.
- Carb Smart mayo saves meaningful calories versus regular mayo with no noticeable flavour difference.
- Add crispy onions from the library if you have a batch — excellent in here.
- For a lower calorie version: one sausage instead of two brings it to ~740 kcal.
