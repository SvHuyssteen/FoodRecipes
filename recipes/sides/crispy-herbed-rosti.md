# Crispy Herbed Rosti

**Category:** Side · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~210 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~4g
**Tags:** side, potato, rosti, air-fryer, herb, vegetarian, crispy

## Ingredients

- 800g roasting potatoes, peeled
- 1 small onion (~100g), grated
- 15g butter, melted
- 10ml olive oil
- 5g fresh rosemary or thyme, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 8g salt
- 3g black pepper

## Method

1. Grate potatoes coarsely — use the Kenwood food processor grater attachment for speed. Feed potatoes through lengthways for longer strands which hold together better. Quarter the onion and run it through the same attachment.
2. Immediately transfer grated potato to a clean tea towel and squeeze out as much moisture as possible. Do it twice, hard. Food processor grating is faster but leaves the potato slightly wetter than hand grating — be extra aggressive here. Salt the grated potato lightly, leave 10 minutes, then squeeze again for even better results if you have time.
3. Squeeze the grated onion out separately in the tea towel too — onion holds a lot of water.
4. Combine potato, onion, melted butter, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper in a bowl. Mix well.
5. Line a flat tray or air fryer shelf with baking paper lightly brushed with olive oil. Press the rosti mixture firmly into individual rounds about 1cm thick and 10cm wide — or one large round if the tray fits. Press hard so they compact and hold together.
6. Brush tops with olive oil. Air fry at 200C for 20-25 minutes, flipping carefully at the halfway mark. Ready when deeply golden and crispy on both sides and a skewer meets no resistance in the centre.
7. Rest 2 minutes before serving — they firm up slightly as they cool.

## Notes

- Squeezing out moisture is everything — wet potato gives you steamed rosti, not crispy rosti.
- Do not use the rotating drum — rosti need to sit flat to form a crust. Use the static tray or shelf.
- If they fall apart when flipping, press back together firmly — they bind as the starch cooks.
- Waxy potatoes hold together better. If yours are floury, add one egg yolk to the mix as a binder.
- Great alongside rotisserie chicken or pork loin — works anywhere you'd serve roast potatoes.
