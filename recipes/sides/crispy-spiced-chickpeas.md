# Crispy Spiced Chickpeas

**Category:** Side · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~180 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~8g
**Tags:** side, chickpeas, middle-eastern, spiced, vegetarian, quick, high-protein, low-cal

## Ingredients

- 400g tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 20ml olive or canola oil
- 5ml cumin
- 3ml coriander
- 3ml smoked paprika
- 2ml chilli flakes
- 3g salt
- Squeeze of lemon juice
- Fresh flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped (optional)

## Method

1. Drain and rinse chickpeas thoroughly. Spread onto a paper towel and pat completely dry. Leave uncovered for 10-15 minutes if you have time — the drier the surface the better. Moisture is the enemy of crisping.
2. Get a wide pan properly hot over medium-high heat before anything goes in. Add the oil — it should shimmer almost immediately.
3. Add chickpeas in a single layer. Leave them completely alone for 2-3 minutes. You should hear active sizzling — if it's quiet the pan isn't hot enough and they're steaming. Turn up the heat.
4. Once they start to colour and blister, toss and leave again. Total pan time 8-10 minutes until golden with a slight crisp shell.
5. Pull the pan off the heat. Add cumin, coriander, paprika, chilli flakes and salt. Toss to coat — adding spices off the heat stops them burning and turning bitter.
6. Squeeze lemon over, scatter parsley if using, and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Canned chickpeas work perfectly — the drying step is what most people skip and why they end up soft and pale.
- Spices go in off the heat only — ground spices burn almost instantly in a dry hot pan.
- Don't crowd the pan or they steam instead of fry. Single layer is essential; do two batches if needed.
- Pairs brilliantly with the Middle Eastern Spiced Pork Chops.
- Also great as a snack or tossed through a simple salad.
