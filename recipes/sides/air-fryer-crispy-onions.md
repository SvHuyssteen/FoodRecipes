# Air Fryer Crispy Onions

**Category:** Side · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~80 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~1g
**Tags:** side, topping, onion, air-fryer, low-cal, quick, make-ahead

## Ingredients

- 2 large onions (~400g), halved and very thinly sliced (2-3mm)
- 15ml olive or canola oil
- 15g cornflour (increase to 20g for weekly batch)
- 3g salt
- 2g baking powder

## Method

1. Slice onions as thin and even as possible — 2-3mm. A mandoline is ideal; otherwise take your time with a sharp knife. Uneven slices mean some burn before others crisp.
2. Toss sliced onions with oil until lightly coated, then add cornflour, salt and baking powder and toss again until evenly dusted. The cornflour pulls surface moisture and creates the crispy coating.
3. Preheat air fryer oven to 180C. Use the drum for small batches or the static rack/tray for larger amounts.
4. Cook at 180C for 15-20 minutes, checking at 12 minutes. They go from golden to burnt quickly toward the end — watch closely in the last few minutes.
5. Tip out onto paper towel immediately and spread out. They crisp up further as they cool — don't leave them in the drum or they'll steam themselves soft.
6. For a weekly batch: leave to air out on the tray for 30 minutes before storing. Store in an airtight jar or container lined with a paper towel in a cool dry cupboard (not the fridge).

## Notes

- Lower temp than chips (180C not 200C) — onions have more sugar and burn faster at high heat.
- They look underdone when you pull them out. Trust the paper towel rest — they harden up noticeably in 2-3 minutes.
- Pull them deep golden rather than light golden if making ahead — more colour means more moisture driven out and longer shelf life.
- Storage life: 2-3 hours at peak crispiness. Properly dried and stored in an airtight jar they last 3-4 days, up to 5 at a stretch.
- For a weekly supply: make two batches — one Sunday, one Wednesday.
- Perfect on burgers, curries, rice dishes, or the rotisserie chicken.
- Airtight container is fine for multi-day storage (paper towel lining handles moisture); avoid airtight for same-day storage as it traps steam.
