# Creamy Parmesan Polenta

**Category:** Side · **Serves:** 4 · **Calories:** ~230 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~6g
**Tags:** side, polenta, parmesan, instant-pot, one-pot, starch-bed, comfort

> The third starch bed alongside the cauliflower mash and rosti — best under anything saucy.
> The Instant Pot removes the only hard part (the stirring).

## Ingredients

- 150g polenta (coarse/traditional beats instant on flavour)
- 500ml water + 250ml milk (or all water + a stock cube for a savoury version)
- 5g salt
- 20g butter
- 30g parmesan, finely grated
- Black pepper

## Method

### Instant Pot (the default)
1. Liquid, salt and polenta into the pot. One good whisk.
2. High pressure 9 minutes, natural release 10.
3. Open and whisk hard 30 seconds — it looks separated, then turns glossy. Whisk in butter and parmesan off the heat. Pepper over.

### Stovetop (if the Instant Pot is busy)
1. Whisk polenta into simmering liquid in a steady stream. Lowest heat, lid mostly on.
2. Stir every 5 minutes for 30-40 minutes until thick and pulling from the sides. Finish with butter and parmesan as above.

## Notes

- Salt like you mean it — polenta, like cauliflower, eats more salt than you expect.
- Consistency is adjustable to the last second: too thick → hot water; too loose → 2 minutes uncovered. Target: slowly settles off a spoon.
- Pairings: under the Bold Pepper Steak Pie filling instead of mash, the lemon chicken traybake's pan juices, the lamb kofta, or anything braised. ~110 kcal more than the cauliflower mash, about level with rice.
- **Second career:** leftover polenta sets solid in the fridge. Next day, slice into batons, spray with oil, air fry at 200C for ~15 minutes → crispy polenta chips (~180 kcal per serve). Salt immediately out of the fryer.
