# Miso Soup

**Category:** Side · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~50 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~4g
**Tags:** side, soup, japanese, miso, low-cal, quick

## Ingredients

- 500ml water (or light stock, or dashi if you ever stock it)
- 30g miso paste
- 100g silken or firm tofu, cubed (optional, adds most of the protein)
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced
- Pinch of dried wakame/seaweed if around (fine without)

## Method

1. Bring the water to a bare simmer. Add tofu (and seaweed) and warm through 2 minutes.
2. OFF the heat: put the miso in a ladle or small sieve, lower it into the liquid and work it through with a spoon until dissolved. Never boil miso — it kills the aroma and the live cultures.
3. Spring onion in, serve.

## Notes

- Five minutes, one pot, ~50 kcal — passes the side-dish rule with room to spare.
- Turns the teriyaki, katsu, oyakodon or glazed fish into a complete Japanese dinner for almost nothing.
- Same miso tub as the glaze and the charred cabbage — one jar, four recipes.
