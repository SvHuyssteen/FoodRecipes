# Peri-Peri Chicken Salad (Leftover Rotisserie)

**Category:** Salad · **Serves:** 1 as a meal · **Calories:** ~380 kcal · **Protein:** ~45g
**Tags:** salad, main, chicken, peri-peri, leftovers, high-protein, low-cal, quick

> Built on leftovers — this is the day-after plan for the
> [Rotisserie Herb & Garlic Chicken](../mains/rotisserie-herb-garlic-chicken.md). Don't cook
> chicken specially for it.

## Ingredients

- ~150g leftover rotisserie chicken, shredded
- 5ml peri-peri sauce (through the chicken) plus more to taste
- 2 big handfuls cos/romaine or whatever leaves are around, torn
- 1/2 cucumber, chopped
- 150g tomatoes, chopped
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced

### Yoghurt peri-peri dressing
- 50g Greek-style yoghurt
- 5ml peri-peri sauce
- Squeeze of lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Toss the shredded chicken with the peri-peri sauce. Cold is fine; 30 seconds in the microwave is also fine.
2. Stir the dressing together — yoghurt, peri-peri, lemon, salt, pepper.
3. Leaves and veg in a bowl, chicken on top, dressing over. One bowl, one small bowl for the dressing, done.

## Notes

- The yoghurt dressing is ~45 kcal where a mayo-based one is ~150 — and it's another job for the yoghurt tub.
- This is the answer to "what's for dinner" the night after rotisserie night — zero cooking, 45g protein.
- Works identically with leftover peri-peri pork loin or the Middle Eastern pork chops (swap peri-peri for a pinch of cumin in the dressing).
