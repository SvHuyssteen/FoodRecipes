# Kachumber

**Category:** Salad · **Serves:** 4 as a side · **Calories:** ~40 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~1g
**Tags:** salad, side, indian, tomato, cucumber, onion, low-cal, quick, no-cook, dal-pairing

## Ingredients

- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
- 1 cucumber (~250g), finely chopped
- 1/2 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
- Small handful fresh coriander, chopped (if it's already in the fridge)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2ml ground cumin
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. Chop everything fine-ish into a bowl.
2. Lemon, cumin, salt. Toss. Done — 5 minutes, one knife, one bowl.

## Notes

- The mandatory cooling side for [Dal Tadka](../mains/dal-tadka.md) — sharp and cold against hot spiced lentils.
- Salt it just before serving; salted early, the tomato and cucumber flood the bowl.
- Basically free food on a cut — eat as much as you like.
