# Tuna & Butter Bean Bowl

**Category:** Salad · **Serves:** 1 as a meal · **Calories:** ~420 kcal · **Protein:** ~38g
**Tags:** salad, main, tuna, butter-beans, high-protein, low-cal, quick, no-cook, pantry

## Ingredients

- 1 tin tuna in brine (~120g drained), drained
- 1 tin butter beans (~230g drained), rinsed
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- Handful of rocket or baby spinach (if in the fridge)
- 10ml olive oil
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Lots of coarse black pepper, salt to taste

## Method

1. Everything into a bowl — tins first, then veg.
2. Oil, lemon, salt, aggressive black pepper. Toss gently so the beans stay whole.
3. Eat. Five minutes, no cooking, and it's a complete dinner.

## Notes

- The actual weeknight meal salad — less work than a Greek salad, 38g protein, entirely from the pantry plus whatever veg is around.
- Butter beans are the heart of it: creamy, substantial, and they carry the dressing. Cannellini work too.
- Variations that stay one-bowl: swap lemon for red wine vinegar, add 30g feta (+110 kcal), or a spoon of pepperdews and their brine for the South African version.
- A tin of smoked mussels or sardines instead of tuna changes the whole mood for the same effort.
