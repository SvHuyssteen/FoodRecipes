# Crispy Chickpea Chopped Salad

**Category:** Salad · **Serves:** 1 as a meal (~550 kcal, ~21g protein) or 2 as a side (~275 kcal) 
**Tags:** salad, main, side, chickpeas, middle-eastern, sumac, vegetarian, high-protein, one-pan, pantry

> The from-scratch alternative to the fattoush: one pan, one bowl. The chickpeas crisp while you
> chop, and they ARE the crunch — no bread, no second component.

## Ingredients

### The one cooked thing
- 400g tin chickpeas, drained, rinsed and patted dry
- 15ml olive oil
- 5ml cumin, 3ml smoked paprika, 2ml chilli flakes, 3g salt

### The chopping
- 1 cucumber, chopped chunky
- 200g tomatoes, chopped
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- Handful flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped

### Dressing
- 10ml olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 5ml sumac (or the lemon's zest + a pinch of smoked paprika)
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Pat the chickpeas dry, get a wide pan hot over medium-high, add the oil, then the chickpeas in a single layer. Leave them alone — they need 8-10 minutes total with an occasional toss.
2. **While the pan works, you chop** — cucumber, tomato, onion, parsley into the bowl; shake the dressing together. By the time you're done, so are the chickpeas.
3. Pull the pan off the heat, toss the spices through the chickpeas (off-heat only — they burn instantly otherwise).
4. Dress the veg, tip the hot chickpeas straight on top, don't fully mix. Hot-crunchy against cold-sharp is the whole dish.

## Notes

- Total effort: one pan, one bowl, ~15 minutes, and the pan time is passive. Same flavour profile as fattoush without the bread production line.
- The chickpeas soften as they sit, so this one is eat-now, not meal-prep.
- 40g feta over the top makes the meal version more substantial (+110 kcal) — the warm chickpeas soften it slightly, which is excellent.
- Entirely from pantry + crisper drawer: no meat, no leftovers required.
