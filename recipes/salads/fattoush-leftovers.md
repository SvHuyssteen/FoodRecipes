# Fattoush (Leftover Collision)

**Category:** Salad · **Serves:** 2 as a side · **Calories:** ~250 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~7g
**Tags:** salad, side, middle-eastern, leftovers, chickpeas, flatbread, sumac

> **This is a leftovers dish, not a project.** It exists for the week you happen to have BOTH a
> spare flatbread AND some [Crispy Spiced Chickpeas](../sides/crispy-spiced-chickpeas.md) from
> pork chop night. Never cook the components specially for it — that's three dishes of effort
> for one side. No leftovers? Make the [Crispy Chickpea Chopped Salad](crispy-chickpea-chopped-salad.md)
> instead — same flavours, one process.

## Ingredients

- 1 leftover flatbread (any of the library's), torn into shards
- A handful or two of leftover crispy spiced chickpeas
- 1 cucumber, chopped chunky
- 200g tomatoes, chopped
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- Handful flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped, plus mint if around

### Dressing
- 15ml olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 5ml sumac (or the lemon's zest + a pinch of smoked paprika)
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Crisp the torn flatbread in the air fryer at 180C for 3-4 minutes while you chop. (Already-stale flatbread is ideal.)
2. Chop the veg into a bowl; whisk the dressing.
3. Dress the veg and herbs, then fold the chickpeas and bread shards through at the last second so they stay crunchy.

## Notes

- Dressing should be sharper than a Greek salad's — the lemon and sumac have to cut the fried bits.
- Assemble at the table, not ahead — fattoush dies by sogginess.
- With 40g feta crumbled over it becomes a light meal (~360 kcal per serve).
