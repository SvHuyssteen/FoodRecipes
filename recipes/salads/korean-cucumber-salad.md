# Korean Cucumber Salad (Oi Muchim)

**Category:** Salad · **Serves:** 2 as a side · **Calories:** ~60 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~2g
**Tags:** salad, side, korean, cucumber, gochugaru, spicy, low-cal, quick, no-cook

## Ingredients

- 1 large cucumber (~300g)
- 5g salt (for drawing out water — mostly drained off)
- 5ml gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes)
- 10ml rice vinegar
- 3ml sesame oil
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- 5g toasted sesame seeds
- Pinch of sugar or allulose

## Method

1. Slice the cucumber into 5mm rounds (or smash it with the flat of the knife first and tear into chunks — more surface, more dressing cling).
2. Toss with the salt, walk away for 10 minutes, then squeeze handfuls firmly over the sink. This is the step that makes it crunchy instead of watery.
3. Toss with everything else. Eat immediately or let it sit 10 minutes — both are correct.
