# Tzatziki

**Category:** Sauce · **Serves:** 4 as a dip/sauce · **Calories:** ~60 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~4g
**Tags:** sauce, greek, mediterranean, yoghurt, cucumber, low-cal, quick, no-cook

## Ingredients

- 250g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 1/2 cucumber, coarsely grated
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- 10ml olive oil
- 10ml lemon juice or red wine vinegar
- 5g fresh dill or mint, chopped (dried dill works)
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Salt the grated cucumber lightly, leave 10 minutes, then squeeze it out HARD in your fist or a tea towel. This is the only real step — unsqueezed cucumber makes tzatziki soup within the hour.
2. Stir everything together. Rest 10 minutes if you can; better after an hour.

## Notes

- Another job for the yoghurt tub — and at ~60 kcal a serve it replaces mayo-based sauces at a quarter of the calories.
- Mandatory alongside the [Chicken Souvlaki](../mains/chicken-souvlaki.md) and [Lamb Kofta](../mains/lamb-kofta.md); also great with the Middle Eastern pork chops.
- Keeps 3-4 days in the fridge; stir before serving.
