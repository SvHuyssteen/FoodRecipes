# All-Purpose Miso Glaze

**Category:** Sauce · **Makes:** 1 batch (~75g, glazes 2-4 fish portions) · **Calories:** ~150 kcal full batch
**Tags:** sauce, japanese, miso, glaze, fish, quick, no-cook

> The shared glaze for the [Kingklip](../mains/miso-glazed-kingklip.md) and
> [Snoek](../mains/miso-glazed-snoek.md) — and it works on chicken thighs, pork chops,
> aubergine or the charred cabbage too.

## Ingredients

- 30g white miso paste
- 20g honey
- 15ml soy sauce
- 10ml rice vinegar (or mirin if you have it)
- 5ml sesame oil (optional, rounds it out)

## Method

1. Whisk everything into a smooth, pourable paste. Loosen with a teaspoon of water if your miso is very stiff. Done — 2 minutes, no cooking.

## Notes

- Keeps 2 weeks in a jar in the fridge; make a double batch.
- Sugar + miso burn fast under heat — glaze goes on in the LAST few minutes of cooking, not the start. Each fish recipe handles the timing.
- Taste your miso first: some are much saltier than others. Adjust honey to match.
