# Korean Soy-Vinegar Dipping Sauce

**Category:** Sauce · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~50 kcal full batch · **Protein:** ~2g
**Tags:** sauce, korean, dipping, soy, vinegar, low-cal, quick, no-cook

## Ingredients

- 30ml dark soy sauce
- 15ml rice vinegar
- 5ml sesame oil
- 5ml gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes)
- 5g toasted sesame seeds
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced

## Method

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl and stir well.
2. Let it sit 5 minutes before serving so the gochugaru softens and the flavours meld.

## Notes

- The classic Korean dumpling dip — sharp, salty and clean. Cuts through rich, crispy or fried food perfectly.
- Designed to pair with the [Korean Gochujang Chicken & Kimchi Rice Paper Parcels](../mains/korean-gochujang-chicken-kimchi-parcels.md) — the acidity contrasts the creamy, rich filling.
- Almost calorie-free, so it adds brightness without affecting your macros.
- Also works as a dip for any dumplings, spring rolls, or as a dressing for a quick cucumber salad.
- Add a small grated garlic clove or a touch of honey if you want to vary it.
