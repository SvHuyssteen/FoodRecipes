# Two-Ingredient Yoghurt Flatbreads

**Category:** Bread · **Serves:** 6 (makes 6 flatbreads) · **Calories:** ~150 kcal per flatbread · **Protein:** ~6g
**Tags:** side, bread, flatbread, curry-pairing, quick, no-yeast

> Recreated October 2026 — the Drive original was a corrupt upload.

## Ingredients

- 200g self-raising flour (or plain flour + 7.5ml baking powder), plus extra for dusting
- 200g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- Pinch of fine salt (technically ingredient three — it's free)

## Method

1. Mix flour and yoghurt (and salt) into a shaggy dough. Knead 2-3 minutes until smooth — soft but not sticky. Different yoghurts carry different moisture; adjust with a spoon of flour or yoghurt as needed.
2. Rest 10-15 minutes covered so the flour hydrates — makes rolling much easier.
3. Divide into 6, roll each to ~3mm on a floured surface.
4. Cook in a dry pan (cast iron ideal) over medium-high heat, ~90 seconds per side, until puffed and charred in spots.
5. Stack under a tea towel as they come off so they stay soft.

## Notes

- The neutral base of the flatbread family — see the [Garlic & Coriander](garlic-coriander-yoghurt-flatbreads.md) and [Sourdough Discard](sourdough-discard-yoghurt-flatbreads.md) variants.
- The pan must be properly hot and dry — oil stops the char spots forming.
- Freeze cooked flatbreads flat with baking paper between; refresh in the air fryer at 180C for 2 minutes.
- Doubles as a pizza-style base — see the Pepperdew, Feta & Pepperoni Flatbread.
