# Sourdough Discard Yoghurt Flatbreads

**Category:** Bread · **Serves:** 6 (makes 6 flatbreads) · **Calories:** ~160 kcal per flatbread · **Protein:** ~6g
**Tags:** side, bread, flatbread, sourdough, discard, yoghurt, curry-pairing, no-yeast

## Ingredients

- 100g sourdough starter discard (unfed is fine)
- 150g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 200g self-raising flour (or plain flour + 7.5ml baking powder), plus extra for dusting
- 5ml fine salt
- Optional: 1 crushed garlic clove + chopped coriander worked into the dough

## Method

1. Mix discard, yoghurt, flour and salt into a shaggy dough. Knead 2-3 minutes until smooth - it should be soft but not sticky; adjust with flour or a splash of yoghurt.
2. Rest 20-30 minutes covered (up to 2 hours at room temp - longer rest = more sourdough tang).
3. Divide into 6, roll each to ~3mm on a floured surface.
4. Cook in a dry pan (cast iron ideal) over medium-high heat, ~90 seconds per side, until puffed and charred in spots.
5. Stack under a tea towel as they come off so they stay soft.

## Notes

- Same role as the Two-Ingredient Yoghurt Flatbreads but the discard adds a lactic tang closer to naan - and it uses up starter you'd otherwise bin.
- The discard makes the dough slightly slacker than the two-ingredient version; flour the bench well.
- Freeze cooked flatbreads flat with baking paper between; refresh in the air fryer at 180C for 2 minutes.
