# Garlic & Coriander Yoghurt Flatbreads

**Category:** Bread · **Serves:** 6 (makes 6 flatbreads) · **Calories:** ~160 kcal per flatbread · **Protein:** ~6g
**Tags:** side, bread, flatbread, garlic, coriander, curry-pairing, quick, no-yeast

> Recreated October 2026 — the Drive original was a corrupt upload.

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 200g self-raising flour (or plain flour + 7.5ml baking powder), plus extra for dusting
- 200g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- Pinch of fine salt

### Garlic-coriander finish
- 15g butter, melted
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- Small handful fresh coriander, finely chopped
- Pinch of flaky salt

## Method

1. Mix the dough ingredients into a shaggy dough. Knead 2-3 minutes until smooth — soft but not sticky; adjust with flour or yoghurt.
2. Rest 10-15 minutes covered.
3. Divide into 6, roll each to ~3mm on a floured surface.
4. Cook in a dry pan (cast iron ideal) over medium-high heat, ~90 seconds per side, until puffed and charred in spots.
5. Stir the grated garlic into the melted butter. Brush each flatbread the moment it comes off the pan — the residual heat takes the raw edge off the garlic. Scatter with coriander and flaky salt.
6. Stack under a tea towel so they stay soft.

## Notes

- Brushing after cooking (not before) keeps the garlic aromatic instead of burnt.
- The go-to pairing for [Dal Tadka](../mains/dal-tadka.md) and anything curried.
- For a leaner version skip the butter and brush with 5ml olive oil mixed with the garlic (~150 kcal).
- Freeze cooked flatbreads flat with baking paper between; refresh in the air fryer at 180C for 2 minutes.
