# Yoghurt Dutch Baby with Honeyed Yoghurt & Berries

**Category:** Breakfast · **Serves:** 2 · **Calories:** ~360 kcal per serve · **Protein:** ~15g
**Tags:** breakfast, dutch-baby, pancake, yoghurt, berries, air-fryer, weekend, balanced

## Ingredients

### Batter
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 60g plain flour
- 60g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 60ml low-fat milk
- 5ml vanilla paste
- Pinch of fine salt
- 10g butter (for the pan)

### Topping
- 100g cultured Greek-style yoghurt
- 10g honey
- 100g mixed berries (fresh or thawed frozen)

## Method

1. Put a 20cm oven-safe skillet or metal cake tin in the Instant Vortex and preheat at 200C for a full 5 minutes - a screaming-hot pan is what makes it puff.
2. Meanwhile blend eggs, flour, yoghurt, milk, vanilla and salt until completely smooth and slightly frothy. (Room-temp eggs matter - cold batter kills the rise.)
3. Drop the butter into the hot pan, swirl to coat, immediately pour in the batter and close the door.
4. Bake at 200C for 15-18 minutes until dramatically puffed at the edges and deep golden. Do not open the door for the first 12 minutes or it collapses.
5. Stir the honey into the topping yoghurt. Serve the dutch baby straight away (it deflates in minutes - that's normal) topped with the honeyed yoghurt and berries.

## Notes

- The yoghurt in the batter adds tang and tenderness; the centre stays slightly custardy, which is correct.
- At ~360 kcal with 15g protein this is a legitimate weekend treat-breakfast, not a blowout - the yoghurt-and-berry topping does the job cream and syrup usually do.
- Savoury variant: drop the vanilla and honey, top with the [Air Fryer Crispy Onions](../sides/air-fryer-crispy-onions.md), a fried egg and chilli flakes.
