# Fish Buying Guide — Cape Town

Researched October 2026. **Hake prices are inflating ~12%/year (~1%/month), so treat these
numbers as a snapshot** — the retailer ranking should age better than the rands.

## The routine

| Fish | Buy from | Price (Oct 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hake** (weekly) | Cape Fish — capefish.co.za | **R119/kg** (1kg packs) | Cheapest verified anywhere incl. supermarkets. R50 delivery, free over R1,000 → order bulk monthly for the freezer. Phone to confirm fresh vs frozen per pack. |
| **Snoek** (in season Sept–Jan) | Greenfish — greenfish.co.za | **~R94–110/kg** (whole flecked, 3–3.5kg, R329) | Handline-caught, Lambertsbaai. Best value AND best provenance. A whole fish = 3–4 meals; portion and freeze. Order by 11am = same day; free collection Maitland. |
| **Kingklip** (treat) | Greenfish first; Shami fallback | Greenfish R389 fresh (~R380/kg); Shami R229/kg frozen | Greenfish is the ONLY seller disclosing catch method (demersal longline = SASSI green). Ask anyone else: "longline or trawl?" — trawl is SASSI orange. |
| **Lazy-week backup** | Checkers / PnP | Sea Harvest hake ~R200/kg; PnP Fishmonger's braai snoek ~R137/kg | Sea Harvest is MSC-certified and frozen-at-sea — genuinely fine. Cape Point 2kg kingklip box hits R250/kg on promo. |

Budget all-rounder: **Shami Seafood** (shamiseafood.com) — hake R130/kg in 5kg boxes, snoek
~R125/kg, kingklip R229/kg, 30 years trading. No sustainability or packing claims though.

## Rules of thumb

- **Sustainability is easy for two of three:** SA hake (MSC re-certified Aug 2026) and snoek are
  SASSI green however caught. **Kingklip flips on catch method** — longline green, offshore trawl
  orange — and almost nobody discloses it. Ask.
- **Ice glaze eats bulk-buy savings:** no SA retailer declares glaze %, and sampling shows frozen
  hake carrying 6–33% ice. A R130/kg box at 20% glaze is really ~R163/kg. Greenfish's 5kg box
  states no-glaze, individually wrapped; most others say nothing. Fresh-counter per-kg is net
  weight — the fresh/frozen gap is smaller than shelf prices suggest.
- **Stock is volatile** at the specialists — when Greenfish has snoek or longline kingklip in
  stock, that's the moment, not next week.
- **Open item:** Woolworths has an orphaned live page for "Hake Fillets 1Kg — R112.99"
  (product 6009182242103) that doesn't appear in their own search. If it rings up at that price
  in store, it's the best hake value in the country; more likely it's a stale delisted line.
